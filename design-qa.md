# Design QA

## Scope

The review covered the responsive English landing page, cursor-reactive aquarium, independently moving fish, rising bubbles, habitat story, fish cards, feature section and footer.

## Visual and interaction checks

- Rounded preview corners, light palette, readable type and responsive fish cards render consistently on desktop and mobile.
- Fish move independently; nearby pointer movement can attract a resident without stopping the others.
- Pause/play, colour mood selection, keyboard movement and fish field notes were verified.
- No horizontal overflow or browser console errors were observed. Images loaded on mobile.
- Production build and available project tests passed.

## Notes

The landing page is a browser simulation, not a capture of the Mac app. Download availability, community destinations and final legal details still need confirmation. Displayed background and performance figures have not been independently measured.
